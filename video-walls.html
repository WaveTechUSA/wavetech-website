Inside a cabinet

Five layers. We know every one

When a wall misbehaves, the fix lives in one of these parts. Knowing which is most of troubleshooting, and why our service calls end quickly.

  1. 1Pixel cardsThe LEDs themselves. Eight per cabinet, and each one lifts off the front without a cable.
  2. 2Hub boardFeeds data and power to every pixel card.
  3. 3Receiving cardsTwo per cabinet. One runs the picture, the other stands by in case it fails.
  4. 4Power suppliesTwo in parallel. If one fails, the other carries the cabinet.
  5. 5CabinetA fan-free housing that doubles as the heat sink, with status lights on the back.
